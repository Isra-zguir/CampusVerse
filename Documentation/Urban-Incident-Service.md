# Urban Incident & Maintenance Management

**Folder:** `Backend/urban-incident-service`  
**Frontend:** `Frontend/urban-incidents`  
**Tech:** Spring Boot 4 · Java 21 · H2 · Angular 21  
**Port:** 8082

Municipal agents and citizens use this service to report, classify, validate and repair urban problems (potholes, broken streetlights, water leaks, etc.).
